Lily’s Puppy
Lily had been wishing for a puppy for a long time. She wanted a silly puppy to run and jump with. But her mummy said Lily could not have one because she was only seven. Lily begged her mummy and daddy. She said, “I am not a baby. It would make me so happy to have a puppy.”
Lily’s mummy and daddy talked for a long time. They said, “We do think you will try your best. We will let you have a puppy.” Lily was so happy. She went to the pet shop and picked a tiny puppy.
