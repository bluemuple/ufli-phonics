A Turtle can Surf!
Irma the turtle loved to surf. Her mother, Shirly, was the first girl surfer in their turtle village, and Irma wanted to be just like her. Every morning Irma would rush to the shoreline.
When she was surfing, Irma would do flips and turns. She would twirl like a windmill. All the other animals were
shocked that a girl turtle could surf so well! Irma would just yell, “A girl can do anything!” just like her mother told her.
Irma would surf until sunset. When the sun was no longer in the sky, she would skip back home. Once she was home, she would curl up in her turtle bed and relax.
