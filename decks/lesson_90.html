The Groom is Late!
The flustered groom rushed into the church. He was very late. Not only was he late, but he had not even finished
dressing. He was still pulling on his boots. He ran to his best man and said, “I slept in! My alarm did not go off!”
“Keep your cool! Better late than never,” said the best man. The groom smoothed his shirt as best as he could and finished pulling on his boots. “All set!” said the groom. “Time to see my bride.” Just then, booming music started to play, and the church doors swung open.
The bride was so pretty as she entered the room, she seemed to be floating. As she reached the groom, she whispered, “Better late than never.” The groom blushed and said, “My alarm did not go off!” With a smile, the bride took her groom’s hand and said, “Next time I will put the alarm on for you.”
