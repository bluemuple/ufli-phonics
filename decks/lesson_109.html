The Baker
Rashawn is a world class baker. He has been baking since he was a little boy. Every day you can find him dancing around the kitchen while making yummy treats. He also loves trading
tips with other bakers about how to make their dishes even better.
One day, Rashawn’s friend, Payton, came over to visit. Payton was hoping to taste some of Rashawn’s newest treats. “You’re right on time!” Rashawn called. “I was just taking a fresh pie out of the oven.” He cut two slices of pie and handed the larger slice to Payton. “I am sure you’re going to love it,”
Rashawn said smiling.
“Yuck!” Payton yelled as she tasted the pie. “What do you mean, yuck?” asked Rashawn, his smile fading, “You don’t like it?”
“Just joking!” Payton said with a grin. “This is the best pie I have ever tasted!” “Oh good,” Rashawn sighed as he sliced the rest of the pie up. “But next time, it would be nicer to say you
liked it from the start.”
