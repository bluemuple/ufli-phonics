The UFLI Foundations decodable text is provided here. See UFLI Foundations decodable text guide for additional text options.
Stink in the Bank
Hank has a job at the bank.
When Hank gets to the bank, he sniffs a bad smell.
“What is that smell?” Hank thinks.
“Is it a skunk?”
He sniffs his desk. “No, it’s not that.”
He checks the fish tank.
“No, this is not what stinks.”
He walks to the back of the bank.
When he gets to the back, he sees the small bin.
“Oh no!” Hank yells, “I left all the stuff in the bin.”
Hank sniffs the bin.
“Yuck!” he yells, “that’s what stinks!”
Hank brings the small bin to the big bin and slams the lid shut.
Hank is glad he got rid of the stink.
