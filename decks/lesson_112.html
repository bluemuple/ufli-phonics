A Smart Pair of Pants
Clare went shopping for a new pair of pants. After going to five stores, she could not find the pair she wanted. Then, at the sixth store, she found them. The pair she picked flared out at the
bottom. They were light as air and so comfortable.
When she put them on, she stared at herself in the mirror. Clare felt so fantastic in these pants that she twirled around and tossed her hair. She wanted to purchase two pairs so she would have a spare pair to wear.
She brought the pants to the checkout counter. “Those are nice pairs of pants,” said the clerk, “if you take good care of
them, they will last forever.” “Thank you,” answered Clare with a big smile, “I sure will.”
