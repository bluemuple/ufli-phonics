The Worst Pet Ever
Morgan has wanted a pet for a long time. One morning, there was a small box on the table with her name on it. Inside the box, there was a bunch of dirt. Under the dirt was a pet worm for her!
Morgan was sad. She thinks a worm is the worst pet in the world. She wanted a dog or cat or even a hamster, not a squirmy worm! What were her mum and
dad thinking? This pet will not work. She started to cry.
Just then, mum and dad came in and said, “We got you! It was a joke! We are going to the pet shop!” Morgan was so happy. She wiped her face and gave
mum and dad a hug. She was going to get the best pet ever!
