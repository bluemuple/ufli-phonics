The Drive Home
Hope and Dad went to the lake for a swim. They swam and swam. “It’s time to go home, Hope,” Dad yelled. As Dad drove home, Hope could see smoke! “Dad there is smoke!” yells Hope. “It is next to the phone shop.”
They drove fast to the phone shop. When they got to the shop they froze. The shop was on fire! Dad calls 000. “Yes, send the fire trucks!” Dad tells the man on the line.
While Dad is on the phone, Hope sees a man run out of the shop. He is safe.
The fire trucks came just in time to get rid of the flames. Hope and Dad save the shop!
