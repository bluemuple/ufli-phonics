A Trip to the Coast
Harvey owns a big yellow boat. Every summer, he takes the boat on a trip to the coast. This time, he wants to bring his friends, Layla and Joe, with him. There is just one problem, Layla can not swim! Layla must be able to swim if she wants to go on the boat.
Layla started swim lessons to grow her skills. She even got a swim coach to teach her. She started slow, but Joe helped her get better. After four weeks of lessons, Layla could swim! Now she could paddle, kick, and float all by herself.
Layla showed Harvey her skills. Harvey was thrilled! The next day, Harvey and Layla and Joe set sail to the coast.
