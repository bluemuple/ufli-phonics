Cave in the Maze
Dale and Jade went into a maze to win a game. The maze was long and full of snakes. Dale said to Jade, “I can not go on.” “Yes, you can. You are brave!” said Jade. Dale and Jade walk and talk.
They made a left and got to the end of the maze. Then they see six small caves. “Let’s go into the sixth cave,” said Jade. In the back of the cave, they see a gate! The gate is shut. Are they stuck?
Dale shakes the gate. Jade kicks the gate. The gate is still shut. “Look! The gate has a lock,” yells Jade. Dale twists the lock. Pop! The gate swings in. Dale and Jade win the game!
