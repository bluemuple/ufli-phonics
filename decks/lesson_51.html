Bring the ring to the shop.

Step 8 · 6-8 minutes · 🔊 = listen without revealing · → = reveal next · click a revealed sentence = read it aloud · S = read · A = all · R = reset
The UFLI Foundations decodable text is provided here. See UFLI Foundations decodable text guide for additional text options.
King and Ash Sing
King and Ash are best pals.
To have fun, King and Ash sing songs.
King brings Ash a book of songs.
“Which song should we sing?” asks King.
“Let’s pick a quick song and then a long song,” said Ash.
King and Ash sing their songs.
King’s mum just got back from the shop.
“Will you sing me a song?” she asks.
“Yes!” yell Ash and King.
They grab the book of songs.
“We have to pick the best song for your mum,” said Ash.
King looks in the book. He sees the best song.
“This is it,” he says. King and Ash sing.
King’s mum claps and says, “I am so glad you sang that song.”
