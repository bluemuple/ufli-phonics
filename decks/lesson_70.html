The Lodge
Calvin has a lodge at the edge of the glen. The lodge has a fridge and a TV. To get to the lodge, you must go on a bridge then go by the ridge next to the lake. You can see many animals move from place to place by the lake.
Calvin loves the lodge. He pledges he will not move from it because his children come to visit him there. Calvin and his children have lots of fun at the lodge. They make music and sing songs by the lake. Sometimes they even jump into the lake and swim with the animals. The lodge is a hidden gem.
