The UFLI Foundations decodable text is provided here. See UFLI Foundations decodable text guide for additional text options.
Clara’s Garden
Clara is an expert at taking care of plants, herbs and ferns.
 
In her garden, Clara plants many herbs and waters them every day. Clara serves herbs to the village when cooking for them. The herbs add a very nice perk to every meal.
At the edge of her garden, Clara sees her purple potted fern. It was close to being an extinct fern so she wanted to preserve it. Next to the fern, she noticed a small herd of slugs. The slugs liked to perch next to the purple fern.
Clara picked up the potted fern as she did not want the slugs to attack her fern. She swerved across the garden and put the potted fern on a ledge.
 
Every herb, fern and plant was important to her. Clara preserved her garden well.
