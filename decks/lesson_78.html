Marvel at the Shore
Cory and his horse, North, went to the shore. Cory held on to North’s mane as North galloped across the sand. They arrived at the shore before anyone else.
There had been a storm at sunset, so this morning the shore was a marvel.
There were hundreds of starfish along the shoreline. The starfish got pulled to the shore in the storm. They were so pretty, but if they were left on the shore they would dry up. Cory jumped off North’s back.
He picked up starfish one by one and placed them back where they came from. Cory was happy he could transport the starfish back home.
