Deep in the Green Forest
Once upon a time, there was a fox who lived in a valley. She spent her days deep in the green forest. Each day, she
would wake up from her sleep and greet the forest animals. She would say hello to the animals in the trees above her. She would also say hello to the animals who rested by the stream.
One morning, the peace of the forest was broken by a horrid scream! The fox looked up to see where the scream
came from. She saw a fallen tree. She peeked in and came upon a monkey. The monkey was stuck in the fallen tree. “Can you please help me get free?” pleaded the monkey. “I will try my best,” the fox said.
The fox pulled very hard and in the end the monkey was freed! “Thank you so much,” the monkey said as he leaped into another tree. As the fox walked back to the valley, she beamed with pride for helping the monkey.
