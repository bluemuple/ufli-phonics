How to use · 사용법
▶ Slides: open in Safari/Chrome, press F for full screen. → next (reveals hidden items first), ← previous, N teacher notes & lesson plan, M jump to a step, S read aloud, E example words for a grapheme, ? all shortcuts.
Click any word or sentence to hear it (Google NZ/AU voice via your Supabase function; falls back to the Mac voice). Grapheme cards in the Visual Drill open example words. Teal HELPER slides were added for the steps that the original slides leave to the manual (phonemic awareness, auditory drill, blending board, spelling, word work, dictation).
📖 Guide: the lesson plan in the manual's format. Click a grapheme (Visual Drill) or a sound (Auditory Drill) for easy example words. Use Print for a PDF.
