Alternate /ā/: Alternate spellings of /ā/: ei, ey, eigh, aigh, ea

Instructional Notes

Low-frequency spellings of /ā/: EI (vein, rein), EY (they, hey, prey, obey), EIGH (eight, weigh, sleigh, neighbour), AIGH (straight), EA (great, break, steak). Many of these words have homophones (rein/rain, weigh/way, great/grate) - discuss meanings.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We know many spellings of /ā/: a (baby), a_e (cake), ai (rain), ay (day). Today we will learn some less common spellings that appear in a few words.

EI spells /ā/ in vein and rein. EY spells /ā/ in they, hey, prey, obey. EIGH spells /ā/ in eight, weigh, sleigh, neighbour. AIGH spells /ā/ in straight. EA spells /ā/ in great, break and steak.

Some of these words sound the same as other words but are spelled differently: rein/rain, weigh/way, great/grate. These are homophones. The spelling tells us which meaning it is.

6: Word Work

Word Meaning Activity (homophones)

Read each pair, then explain the meaning of each word: rein/rain, wait/weight, waist/waste, way/weigh, great/grate, brake/break, steak/stake, eight/ate, prey/pray.

vein → rein → rain → ray → prey → grey → hey → they → weigh → sleigh → neigh → eight → weight → freight → straight → great → break → steak

reinReins are the thin leather straps attached around a horse's neck for horseback riding.
rainRain is water that falls from the clouds in small drops.
waitWhen you wait for something or someone, you spend time doing very little, because you cannot act until that thing happens or that person arrives.
weightWeight is how heavy something is, measured in units like grams or kilograms.

Greyson’s Neighborhood

Greyson lives in a beige house on the corner of Eighth Street and Great Lake Road. He has lived there for eighteen years. Greyson loves his neighbourhood because he has great neighbours. Any time he passes someone on his street they always wave and say hey. Greyson’s next-door neighbour is Corey. Corey loves to cook and has a huge grill in his backyard. Every Friday, Corey hosts a dinner party for Greyson and some other neighbours. Last Friday, Corey grilled steak and corn. It was quite tasty! Greyson’s neighbours across the street are Cosmo and Wanda. They have three dogs, four cats, and one fish. That’s eight pets! Sometimes, Greyson walks the dogs for Cosmo and Wanda if they need a break. When Greyson walks the dogs, they always go straight to the park to play fetch. Greyson loves his beige house, his nice neighbourhood, and his friendly neighbours. He thinks he just might stay there for another eighteen years.
