5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter D. D is a consonant. The letter D spells the /d/ sound like at the beginning of dog.

D can come at the beginning of a word, such as dip and dad. D can also come at the end of a word, such as mad and pad.

Grapheme placement (slides)

dip/dad/d ; mad/pad/d

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /d/ sound as in 'dog', put the tip of your tongue behind your top teeth and pop the air out with your voice on... (model). It is like /t/ but with the voice on. /d/ is a quick, stop sound. Watch me /d/. You try /d/.

Sound Wall

Add 'd' under /d/ (tongue behind top teeth).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase D and lowercase d.
