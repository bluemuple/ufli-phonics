Instructional Notes

u_e spells two sounds: /ū/ as in rule, flute, and /yū/ as in cute, mule, use. When reading, try /yū/ first; if it does not make a word, try /ū/.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We have learned the VCe pattern with a_e, i_e, o_e and e_e. Today we will learn u_e. U, consonant, silent e can spell two sounds.

Sometimes u_e says /ū/ as in rule and flute. Sometimes u_e says /yū/ as in cute and mule. The /yū/ sound is the name of the letter U. When you read a u_e word, try /yū/ first; if it does not sound like a real word, try /ū/.

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /ū/ sound as in 'spoon', round your lips tightly... (model). To make /yū/ as in 'unicorn', start with /y/ and glide into /ū/. Both sounds have the voice on. Watch me /ū/, /yū/. You try.

Sound Wall

Add 'u_e' under /ū/ and under /yū/ on the vowel valley.
