Long VCC: Long VCC patterns: -ild, -old, -ind, -olt, -ost

Instructional Notes

In a few endings the vowel is long even though the syllable is closed: wild, child, mild; old, cold, told; find, kind, mind; bolt, colt; most, post, host. Exceptions: wind (air), cost, lost, frost, gold vs. golf.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We know that in a closed syllable the vowel is usually short: hot, hop. Today we will learn some special endings where the vowel is long even though the syllable is closed.

-ild says /īld/: wild, child, mild. -ind says /īnd/: find, kind, mind. -old says /ōld/: old, cold, told. -olt says /ōlt/: bolt, colt. -ost says /ōst/: most, post, host.

Learn these endings as chunks. Be careful: some words with these letters keep the short vowel - cost, lost, frost, wind (the blowing kind). Be flexible: try the long sound first, then the short sound.
