5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter B. B is a consonant. The letter B spells the /b/ sound like at the beginning of bee.

B can come at the beginning of a word, such as bat and big. B can also come at the end of a word, such as cab and tub.

Grapheme placement (slides)

bat/big/b ; cab/tub/b

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /b/ sound as in 'bee', put your lips together and pop them open with your voice on... (model). It is like /p/ but with the voice on. /b/ is a quick, stop sound. Watch me /b/. You try /b/.

Sound Wall

Add 'b' under /b/ (lips together).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase B and lowercase b. Contrast b and d.
