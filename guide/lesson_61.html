_ge /j/: _ge spells /j/ (soft g)

Instructional Notes

G spells /j/ when followed by e (also i and y - Lesson 117). Words ending in -ge: cage, page, huge. J is never used at the end of a word; -ge is used instead (and -dge after a short vowel, Lesson 70).

5: New Concept

Introduction

We know that G spells /g/ as in goat. Today we will learn that G can also spell /j/. When G is followed by E, it usually spells /j/, like at the end of page and cage.

-ge comes at the end of a word: cage, huge, stage. The e is silent. It tells the G to say /j/, and it usually makes the vowel long: c-a-ge, cage. After N the vowel stays short or changes: range, change, hinge.

Remember: we never write J at the end of a word. We use -ge.

Articulatory Gesture

Review the /j/ gesture (teeth together, lips round, voice on, quick).
