Instructional Notes

L at the end of a one-syllable word after a short vowel is usually doubled (bell, hill) - that comes in Lesson 42. Today L is at the beginning or after another sound (help, milk).

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter L. L is a consonant. The letter L spells the /l/ sound like at the beginning of leaf.

L can come at the beginning of a word, such as lap and let. L can also come in the middle or at the end of a word, such as help and milk.

Grapheme placement (slides)

lap/let/l

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /l/ sound as in 'leaf', put the tip of your tongue behind your top teeth and let your voice flow around the sides of your tongue... (model). Be sure your voice is on. /l/ is a continuous sound. Watch me /lll/. You try /lll/.

Sound Wall

Add 'l' under /l/ (tongue behind top teeth).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase L and lowercase l.
