wh /w/, ph /f/: The digraphs WH /w/ and PH /f/

Instructional Notes

WH spells /w/ at the beginning of words (when, which, whip). In who, WH spells /h/ (heart word, Lesson 54). PH spells /f/ in words from Greek (phone, graph).

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we will learn two more digraphs. The digraph WH spells /w/, the same sound as W, like at the beginning of whip and when. WH comes at the beginning of a word. Many question words start with wh: what, when, which, where, why.

The digraph PH spells /f/, the same sound as F, like in phone and graph. PH can come at the beginning or end of a word. Words with PH come from Greek.

Articulatory Gesture

Review the gestures for /w/ (lips rounded, voice on) and /f/ (teeth on lip, voice off).

On a Whim
