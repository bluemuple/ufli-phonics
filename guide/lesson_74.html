y /ē/: The grapheme Y spells /ē/ at the end of a word

Instructional Notes

At the end of a two-syllable word, Y spells /ē/: happy, funny, baby. Divide the syllables, then read: hap-py, ba-by.

5: New Concept

Introduction

Yesterday we learned that Y spells /ī/ at the end of a short word: my, fly. Today we will learn that at the end of a longer word, Y spells /ē/, like at the end of happy and baby.

Y /ē/ comes at the end of two-syllable words: hap-py, fun-ny, ba-by. To read these words, divide the syllables, read each part, then blend: fun / ny, funny.

Articulatory Gesture

Review the /ē/ gesture.
