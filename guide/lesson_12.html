5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter O. O is a vowel. The letter O spells the /ŏ/ sound like at the beginning of octopus.

O can come at the beginning of a word, such as on. O can also come in the middle of a word, such as top and mop.

Grapheme placement (slides)

top/mop/o ; on/o

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /ŏ/ sound as in 'octopus', open your mouth wide and round like an O... (model). Be sure your voice is on. /ŏ/ is a continuous sound that can be stretched out. Watch me /ŏŏŏ/. You try /ŏŏŏ/.

Sound Wall

Add 'o' under /ŏ/ on the vowel valley.

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase O and lowercase o.
