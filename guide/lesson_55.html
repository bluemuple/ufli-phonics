5: New Concept

Introduction

We learned that silent e makes A say its name: cape. Today we will learn that the VCe pattern works with I too. I, consonant, silent e: the I says /ī/, its name, like in kite.

Look: bit. Add the silent e: bite. b-i-t-e, bite. The I is long. Words with i_e: ride, like, time, five.

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /ī/ sound as in 'ice cream', open your mouth wide and glide to a small smile... (model). Be sure your voice is on. /ī/ is a continuous sound. Watch me /īīī/. You try /īīī/.

Sound Wall

Add 'i_e' under /ī/ on the vowel valley.
