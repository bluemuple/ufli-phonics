z /z/: The grapheme Z spells /z/

Instructional Notes

If a word has a short vowel followed by /z/, the /z/ is usually spelled zz (buzz). This is introduced in Lesson 42 (FLSZ rule).

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter Z. Z is a consonant. The letter Z spells the /z/ sound like at the beginning of zip. We already know that S can spell /z/ too (is, dogs).

Z comes at the beginning of a word, such as zip and zap. Z also comes at the end of a few words, such as quiz.

Grapheme placement (slides)

zip/zap/z ; quiz/z

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /z/ sound as in 'zip', put your teeth together, keep your lips open and buzz with your voice on... (model). It is like /s/ with the voice on. /z/ is a continuous sound. Watch me /zzz/. You try /zzz/.

Sound Wall

Add 'z' under /z/ next to 's'.

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase Z and lowercase z.
