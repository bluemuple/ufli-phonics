ee, ea, ey /ē/: The vowel teams EE, EA and EY spell /ē/

Instructional Notes

EE and EA spell /ē/ at the beginning, middle or end of a word (eel, feet, see; eat, team, sea). EY spells /ē/ at the end of a word, often in two-syllable words (key, monkey, turkey).

EA also spells /ĕ/ (head) and /ā/ (great) - Lessons 94 and 114.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We know that /ē/ can be spelled e in an open syllable (be, began) and e_e (these). Today we will learn three vowel teams that spell /ē/.

EE spells /ē/ like in feet and see. EA spells /ē/ like in eat and team. Both can come at the beginning, middle or end of a word. EY spells /ē/ at the end of a word, like key and monkey. It is often at the end of a two-syllable word: mon-key, tur-key.

When you spell /ē/ in the middle of a word, it could be ee or ea - you have to remember which.

Decodable Text (from the slides)

Deep in the Green Forest

Once upon a time, there was a fox who lived in a valley. She spent her days deep in the green forest. Each day, she would wake up from her sleep and greet the forest animals. She would say hello to the animals in the trees above her. She would also say hello to the animals who rested by the stream. One morning, the peace of the forest was broken by a horrid scream! The fox looked up to see where the scream came from. She saw a fallen tree. She peeked in and came upon a monkey. The monkey was stuck in the fallen tree. “Can you please help me get free?” pleaded the monkey. “I will try my best,” the fox said. The fox pulled very hard and in the end the monkey was freed! “Thank you so much,” the monkey said as he leaped into another tree. As the fox walked back to the valley, she beamed with pride for helping the monkey.
