Closed & Open Syllables: Closed and open syllables

Instructional Notes

A syllable is a word or word part with one vowel sound. A closed syllable ends in a consonant and the vowel is short (hop, kick). An open syllable ends in a vowel and the vowel is long - it says its name (go, hi, me, we, she).

This lesson makes he, be, me, we, she, go, no, so decodable.

5: New Concept

Introduction

A syllable is a word or part of a word with one vowel sound. Clap the syllables: cat (1), rabbit (2), banana (3).

A closed syllable has a vowel followed by at least one consonant. The consonant 'closes in' the vowel, and the vowel is short: hop, kick, just. All the CVC words we know are closed syllables.

An open syllable ends with the vowel. Nothing closes it in, so the vowel is long - it says its name: go, hi, me, we, she, so, no. Say the sounds: /g/ /ō/, go.

Knowing whether a syllable is open or closed helps us read the vowel.

Articulatory Gesture

Review the long vowel sounds /ā/ /ē/ /ī/ /ō/ /ū/ (the letter names).

Sound Wall

Add cards for open-syllable vowels: a /ā/, e /ē/, i /ī/, o /ō/, u /ū/ /yū/ on the vowel valley.
