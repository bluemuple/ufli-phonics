j /j/: The grapheme J spells /j/

Instructional Notes

J is only used at the beginning of words and syllables. /j/ at the end of a word is spelled -ge or -dge (Lessons 61 and 70).

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter J. J is a consonant. The letter J spells the /j/ sound like at the beginning of jar.

J comes at the beginning of a word, such as job and jet. J is not used at the end of a word.

Grapheme placement (slides)

job/jet/j

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /j/ sound as in 'jar', put your teeth together and round your lips, then push the air out with your voice on... (model). It is like /ch/ with the voice on. /j/ is a quick sound. Watch me /j/. You try /j/.

Sound Wall

Add 'j' under /j/ (tongue lifted).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase J and lowercase j.
