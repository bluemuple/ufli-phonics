Closed/Closed: Two-syllable words: closed/closed

Instructional Notes

Divide two-syllable words between the two consonants (VC/CV): rab-bit, den-tist. Both syllables are closed, so both vowels are short. Read each syllable, then blend them.

The vowel in an unstressed syllable may sound like schwa /ə/ (second, absent). Accept the relaxed pronunciation.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We have been reading one-syllable words and compound words. Today we will read other two-syllable words. Look at this word: rabbit. It has two vowels with two consonants between them: r-a-b-b-i-t.

We divide the word between the two consonants: rab / bit. Each part is a closed syllable, so each vowel is short. Read the first syllable: rab. Read the second syllable: bit. Blend them: rabbit.

When you see a long word, find the vowels, divide between the consonants, read each syllable, then put them together.
