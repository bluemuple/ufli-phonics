Instructional Notes

C spells /k/ before a, o and u (cat, cot, cup). The letter K is introduced in Lesson 22 for /k/ before i and e.

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter C. C is a consonant. The letter C spells the /k/ sound like at the beginning of cat.

C comes at the beginning of a word before a, o or u, such as can and cat. C does not usually spell /k/ at the end of a word - we will learn other spellings later.

Grapheme placement (slides)

can/cat/c

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /k/ sound as in 'kite', pull the back of your tongue up to the back of the roof of your mouth and pop the air out... (model). Your voice is off. /k/ is a quick, stop sound. Watch me /k/. You try /k/.

Sound Wall

Add 'c' under /k/ (tongue pulled back).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase C and lowercase c.
