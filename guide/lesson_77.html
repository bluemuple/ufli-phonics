Instructional Notes

AR is an r-controlled vowel: the R changes the vowel sound so A no longer says /ă/. In Australian/NZ English /ar/ is a long open vowel with no /r/ sound at the end (car, park).

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letters AR. We know A spells /ă/. When A is followed by R, the R changes the vowel sound. AR spells /ar/, like in the words bark, far and arm. We call this an r-controlled vowel: the R is the boss of the vowel.

AR can come at the beginning of a word, such as art and arm. AR can come in the middle, such as bark and part, or at the end, such as far and star.

Grapheme placement (slides)

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /ar/ sound as in 'arm', open your mouth wide and relaxed, and drop your chin a little... (model). Be sure your voice is on. /ar/ is a continuous sound that can be stretched out. Watch me /ar/. You try /ar/.

The Garden

Omar loves to be in his garden on his farm. While it is still dark, he drags his old cart across the yard to the barn. He fills the cart with plants for the garden. When he gets to the garden, he looks at the plants one by one. Then he finds them all the best place to live. He finds big spaces for the large plants. He finds shade for the plants that do not like the harsh sun. He gives them all just what they want and makes them look so pretty. When Omar is done, he brings the cart back to the barn.
