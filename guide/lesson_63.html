-es: The suffix -es

Instructional Notes

Add -es (not -s) after words ending in s, x, z, sh, ch, ss: box → boxes, dish → dishes. The suffix -es adds a syllable: /ĕz/ or /əz/.

-es also changes verbs: he fixes, she wishes.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We learned that the suffix -s makes nouns plural (more than one) and changes verbs (he runs). Today we learn the suffix -es. When a word ends in s, x, z, sh or ch, we add -es instead of -s: one box, two boxes; one dish, two dishes.

-es adds a new syllable to the word: box-es, dish-es. Listen: /bŏks/ /ĕz/. We also add -es to verbs: I wish, she wishes; I fix, he fixes.

Articulatory Gesture

No new sound. Notice that -es is a whole syllable: /ĕz/.

Sound Wall

Add '-es' as a suffix card.
