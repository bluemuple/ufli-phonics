5: New Concept

Introduction

We have learned the VCe pattern with a_e, i_e and o_e. Today we will review those and learn e_e. E, consonant, silent e: the E says /ē/, its name, like in these.

Not many words use e_e: these, theme, eve, Pete, Steve, complete. Most words with the /ē/ sound use other spellings that we will learn later.

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /ē/ sound as in 'eagle', smile wide with your teeth close together... (model). Be sure your voice is on. /ē/ is a continuous sound. Watch me /ēēē/. You try /ēēē/.

Sound Wall

Add 'e_e' under /ē/ on the vowel valley.

The Long Hike

Chan rides his skates to the lake. When Chan gets to the lake, he looks for his pal Eve. He sees Eve on the black dock. “Want to hike?” asks Eve. “Yep!” Chan says. “OK. Grab these snacks for us to have on the hike,” says Eve. Eve and Chan hike for a long time. “This is a long hike!” says Chan. “Yes. It is long” says Eve. Eve and Chan talk as they walk. Eve tells Chan when they are close to the top. “Here we are! The top of the hill!” Eve yells. Eve and Chan can see a lot. “Thank you for this grand hike,” says Chan with a wide smile.
