un-: The prefix un-

Instructional Notes

A prefix is a morpheme added to the beginning of a word. un- means 'not' or 'the opposite of' (unhappy = not happy; unzip = the opposite of zip).

5: New Concept

Introduction

A suffix is a morpheme added to the end of a word. A prefix is a morpheme added to the beginning of a word. Today we will learn the prefix un-.

un- means 'not' or 'the opposite of'. Unhappy means not happy. Unkind means not kind. Unzip means the opposite of zip. Uncover means the opposite of cover.

Read the prefix, then the base word, then think about the meaning: un-lock, unlock - the opposite of lock.

Decodable Text (from the slides)

Unlucky

Something very unlucky happened to Lee last week. As he was walking into his classroom, he did not see the puddle on the floor. He slid and fell before his entire class. That’s not even the worst part! Lee was holding a jar of marbles with the lid unscrewed. When he fell, the marbles flew everywhere. Lee laid on the floor, hid his face and wanted to cry. But then, all of a sudden, his best friend, Jayden, sat down next to him. Jayden took Lee’s hands off his face and helped him up. “Don’t worry,” Jayden said, “people fall all the time.” At first, Lee felt uneasy. When he looked around at his classmates, nobody made fun of him for falling. Instead, Lee’s classmates began picking up all the marbles that spilled. Some of them even told him about times when mistakes like this happened to them. It turns out, it is not that uncommon to slip and fall. “I am thankful to have such nice friends,” Lee said.
