FLSZ Spelling Rule: FLSZ spelling rule: ff, ll, ss, zz

Instructional Notes

The FLSZ rule: when a one-syllable word has a short vowel followed by /f/, /l/, /s/ or /z/ at the end, the final letter is doubled (off, hill, miss, buzz). The doubled letter spells one sound.

Word work today uses writing, because most manipulative letter sets have only one of each letter.

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning a spelling rule. When a short word has a short vowel and ends with the sound /f/, /l/, /s/ or /z/, we usually double the last letter: off, hill, miss, buzz.

The two letters spell one sound. When you read, say the sound once: /ŏf/, off. When you spell, remember the rule: FLSZ - f, l, s and z are doubled after a short vowel at the end of a word.

Remember that some words are exceptions, such as bus, gas, yes and if.

Articulatory Gesture

Review the gestures for /f/, /l/, /s/ and /z/. Point out that /f/ and /s/ are quiet, /l/ and /z/ have the voice on.

Sound Wall

Add 'ff' under /f/, 'll' under /l/, 'ss' under /s/ and 'zz' under /z/.
