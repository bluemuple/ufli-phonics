y /ī/: The grapheme Y spells /ī/ at the end of a word

Instructional Notes

Y is a vowel at the end of a word. In one-syllable words (and stressed endings) it says /ī/: my, fly, sky. This makes by and my decodable.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We know that Y is a consonant at the beginning of a word, as in yes and yellow. Today we will learn that Y is a vowel at the end of a word. At the end of a short word, Y spells /ī/, like in my and fly.

Y /ī/ comes at the end of one-syllable words: by, my, sky, try, why. English words do not usually end with the letter I, so Y does the job: fl-y, fly.
