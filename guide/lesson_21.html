-s /z/: The suffix -s spells /z/

Instructional Notes

After voiced sounds (like /g/, /n/, /d/, /b/ and vowels) the suffix -s says /z/: dogs, pens, fans. Students already know S spells /s/; today S also spells /z/ (is, as, has).

5: New Concept

Introduction

We learned that the suffix -s says /s/ after quiet sounds like /t/ and /p/: cats, maps. Today we will learn that after noisy (voiced) sounds, the suffix -s says /z/: dogs, pens, bugs.

Listen: /dŏg/ - /dŏgz/. The voice stays on, so the -s sounds like /z/. The letter S can spell /s/ and /z/. We also see S spelling /z/ in the words is, as and has.

Articulatory Gesture

Compare /s/ (voice off) and /z/ (voice on) with a hand on the throat. Both use teeth together with air pushed through.

Sound Wall

Add 's' and '-s' under /z/.
