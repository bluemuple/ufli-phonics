r /r/ Part 2: R in consonant clusters at the beginning of words (br, cr, dr, fr, gr, pr, tr)

Instructional Notes

A consonant cluster (often called a 'blend') is two consonants together that keep their own sounds: /k/ /r/ in crab. Do not teach clusters as a single sound - segment each sound.

The /t/ before /r/ often sounds like /ch/ (trip) and /d/ before /r/ like /j/ (drum). This is normal; students still spell them with t and d.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We know that R spells /r/ at the beginning of a word. R can also come right after another consonant at the beginning of a word: c-r-a-b, crab. Each consonant keeps its own sound, so we say every sound: /k/ /r/ /ă/ /b/.

Today we will read and spell words that begin with two consonants, such as grab, frog, crop and trip. Listen carefully for both sounds at the start.

Articulatory Gesture
