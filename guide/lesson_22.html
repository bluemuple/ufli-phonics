k /k/: The grapheme K spells /k/

Instructional Notes

K is used to spell /k/ before i and e (kid, kept). C is used before a, o and u. Both letters spell the same sound.

ask and fast are heart words for now: in Australian/NZ English the A says /ar/ (introduced in Lesson 77 AUS).

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter K. K is a consonant. The letter K spells the /k/ sound like at the beginning of kite. We already know that C spells /k/ too.

K comes at the beginning of a word before i and e, such as kid and kit. C comes before a, o and u, such as cat and cot. K can also come at the end of a word after another consonant, such as desk.
