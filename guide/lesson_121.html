-er, -or, -ist: The suffixes -er, -or and -ist mean some(one/thing) that does an action

Instructional Notes

The focus of this lesson is on the meaning of the suffixes and their role in changing the part of speech. The concepts of nouns and verbs should be addressed separately, as needed.

In Lesson 100, students learned about the suffix -ER when used with adjectives to mean 'more than' (e.g., stronger). In this lesson, -ER is used to change verbs to nouns (e.g., teach → teacher).

5: New Concept

Introduction

Remember, a morpheme is a word part that changes a word's meaning. A suffix is a morpheme that comes at the end of a word. Today we will learn about three new suffixes: -ER, -OR, and -IST.

-er: We already know that when we add the suffix -ER to adjectives, it means more than like in the word bigger. When we add the suffix -ER to verbs, or action words, it makes the verb become a noun meaning one that does. Teach is a verb. Adding -ER to teach makes teacher. A teacher is a person who teaches. Print is a verb. Adding -ER makes printer. A printer is something that prints.

-or, -ist: -OR and -IST are also used to make verbs into nouns. -OR is pronounced /er/ (/ə/) as in sailor. -IST is pronounced /ist/ as in cartoonist. A sailor is someone who sails. A cartoonist is someone who draws cartoons.

Discuss how suffixes impact word meanings as you read and spell.

Decodable Text (from the slides)

Chris Joins a Club

Chris wants to join a club. She wants to learn a new skill and make new friends. The only problem is that Chris has no idea what club to join! Luckily, there is a showcase after school where Chris can learn about all the clubs. The club showcase is in the gym. There are rows and rows of tables with members of all the different clubs. First, Chris visits the table for Art Club. The Art Club members are sculptors, painters, and cartoonists. Chris thought the art they created was nice, but she does not want to be an artist. Next, Chris visits the table for Sports Club. In Sports Club, you work with athletic trainers. You can learn to be a basketball player or even a baseball catcher. Chris thought of herself as more of a sports watcher than a sports player. This is not the club for her. Finally, Chris stops at the table for Garden Club. In Garden Club, you learn how to grow and care for plants from real botanists. A botanist is someone who studies plants. You even work with real farmers to learn how to grow your own food. Chris has always wanted to learn to garden. This is the perfect club for her. She may even become a botanist or a farmer one day.
