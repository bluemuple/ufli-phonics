Instructional Notes

Y is a consonant at the beginning of a word (yes). At the end of words Y is a vowel (my, happy) - Lessons 73 and 74.

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter Y. Y is a consonant at the beginning of a word. The letter Y spells the /y/ sound like at the beginning of yellow.

Y comes at the beginning of a word, such as yes and yam.

Grapheme placement (slides)

yes/yam/y

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /y/ sound as in 'yellow', lift the middle of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth and glide your voice out... (model). Be sure your voice is on. Watch me /y/. You try /y/.

Sound Wall

Add 'y' under /y/ (tongue lifted).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase Y and lowercase y.
