v /v/: The grapheme V spells /v/

Instructional Notes

English words do not end in v on its own - a silent e is added (give, have). That is why have is a heart word for now.

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter V. V is a consonant. The letter V spells the /v/ sound like at the beginning of volcano.

V comes at the beginning of a word, such as van and vet. V does not come at the very end of a word - English words add an e after v (have, give).

Grapheme placement (slides)

van/vet/v

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /v/ sound as in 'volcano', put your top teeth on your bottom lip and let the air out with your voice on... (model). It is like /f/ with the voice on. /v/ is a continuous sound. Watch me /vvv/. You try /vvv/.

Sound Wall

Add 'v' under /v/ (teeth on lip).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase V and lowercase v.
