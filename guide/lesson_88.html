5: New Concept

Introduction

We have learned vowel teams for /ā/ (ai, ay), /ē/ (ee, ea, ey), /ō/ (oa, ow, oe) and /ī/ (ie, igh). Today we review all of them and read longer words that use them: rain-coat, day-light, be-tween.

Look for the vowel team in each syllable. Read each syllable, then blend.

Decodable Text (from the slides)

Aiden’s Goal

Aiden had to find a way to make money. He needed to save up for his dream car. He wanted to get a jeep, but jeeps are not cheap. He needed to find a job that paid very well, so he could own his dream car. What could he do? Aiden went to his father for advice. His father said he should study. “How will studying get me my dream car?” Aiden asked. His father explained that he could study a trade like welding or carpentry. He could also take more classes to get a degree. People who had a trade or a degree could make a lot of money. Aiden needed to think over what his father had told him. It would take longer to study, but in the end, Aiden would be able to get his dream car and more. After days of thinking, Aiden chose to take more classes. This would help him reach his dream and his next one, whatever that might be. Aiden’s father was almost always right.
