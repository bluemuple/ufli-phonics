x /ks/: The grapheme X spells /ks/

Instructional Notes

X spells two sounds, /k/ + /s/. It usually comes at the end of a word (box, six). Count /ks/ as two sounds when segmenting.

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter X. The letter X spells two sounds together, /k/ and /s/, like at the end of box.

X comes at the end of a word, such as box and wax. It can also come before t, such as next and text.

Grapheme placement (slides)

box/wax/x

Articulatory Gesture

Say /k/ then /s/ quickly: /ks/. Feel the back of the tongue pop for /k/ then the air hiss for /s/. Watch me /ks/. You try /ks/.

Sound Wall

Add 'x' under /k/ and /s/ with a note that it spells both sounds.

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase X and lowercase x.
