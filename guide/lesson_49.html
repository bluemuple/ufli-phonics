5: New Concept

Introduction

We have learned four consonant digraphs: CK /k/, SH /sh/, TH /th/ and /th/, and CH /ch/. A digraph is two letters that spell one sound. Today we practise reading and spelling words with all of them.

When you see two consonants together, check: is it a digraph (one sound) or a cluster (two sounds)? sh-i-p (3 sounds), s-l-i-p (4 sounds).

Snacks for the Pets

Beth has a lot of pets. She has ten small chicks and six big sloths. She grabs a big bag of snacks and drags it to the pet pens. The ten small chicks run to Beth. “Do you chicks want a snack?” Beth asks. “Cluck, cluck, cluck,” go the chicks. The chicks peck at their snacks. Beth looks up at the sloths. “Do you sloths want a snack?” The sloths nod. Beth hands the sloths their dish of snacks. Munch, munch, munch go the sloths. Beth grabs a snack from the bag. “I will have this snack,” Beth says. Beth sits and snacks with the pets.
