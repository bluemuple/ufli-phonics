5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter N. N is a consonant. The letter N spells the /n/ sound like at the beginning of nose.

N can come at the beginning of a word, such as nut and nod. N can also come at the end of a word, such as in and pin.

Grapheme placement (slides)

nut/nod/n ; in/pin/n

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /n/ sound as in 'nose', put the tip of your tongue behind your top teeth and let the air come out of your nose... (model). Be sure your voice is on. /n/ is a continuous sound. Watch me /nnn/. You try /nnn/.

Sound Wall

Add 'n' under /n/ (tongue behind top teeth).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase N and lowercase n.
