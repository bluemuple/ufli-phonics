Voiced th /th/: The consonant digraph TH spells voiced /th/

Instructional Notes

TH spells two sounds. Today's words use voiced /th/ (voice on) as in this and that. Unvoiced /th/ (thin, bath) is Lesson 47. Many high-frequency words use voiced th: the, this, that, them, then, than, they, their.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We know that a consonant digraph is two consonants that come together to make one sound, like SH. Today we will learn the digraph TH. T and H together make a new sound.

TH can spell two sounds. Today we learn the /th/ sound with your voice on, like in the words this and that. Put your tongue between your teeth and turn your voice on.

TH comes at the beginning of many small words we use all the time: the, this, that, them, then, than. TH /th/ can also come at the end of a few words, such as with (in some accents) and smooth.

Grapheme placement (slides)

them/that/th

Articulatory Gesture

To make the voiced /th/ sound, put your tongue between your teeth and push air out with your voice on... (model). Feel the buzz on your tongue. /th/ is a continuous sound. Watch me /th/. You try /th/.

Sound Wall

Add 'th' under voiced /th/ (tongue between teeth).

Letter Formation
