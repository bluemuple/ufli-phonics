Instructional Notes

/er/ has several spellings: er, ir, ur, and or after w (work, word, world). When spelling, use these hints: er is the most common and is used at the end of two-syllable words; ur/ir are common in one-syllable words; after w, /er/ is often spelled or.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We know that /er/ can be spelled er, ir and ur. Today we will learn one more spelling. After the letter W, the sound /er/ is usually spelled OR: work, word, world, worm. The W makes OR say /er/ instead of /or/.

When you spell a word with /er/, think about which spelling it uses: her (er), bird (ir), turn (ur), work (w + or). We will sort words by their spelling today.

The Worst Pet Ever

Morgan has wanted a pet for a long time. One morning, there was a small box on the table with her name on it. Inside the box, there was a bunch of dirt. Under the dirt was a pet worm for her! Morgan was sad. She thinks a worm is the worst pet in the world. She wanted a dog or cat or even a hamster, not a squirmy worm! What were her mum and dad thinking? This pet will not work. She started to cry. Just then, mum and dad came in and said, “We got you! It was a joke! We are going to the pet shop!” Morgan was so happy. She wiped her face and gave mum and dad a hug. She was going to get the best pet ever!
