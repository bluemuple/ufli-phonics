Compound Words: Compound words

Instructional Notes

A compound word is two smaller words joined to make a new word (sun + set = sunset). Reading longer words: find the two small words, read each, then put them together.

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we will learn about compound words. A compound word is made of two smaller words put together: sun + set = sunset. Both small words keep their spelling and their meaning.

To read a long word, look for two small words you know. Read the first word, read the second word, then blend them: back-pack, backpack. Compound words have two syllables (or more), one for each small word.

Mo and the Sandpit

Mo was at home with his nan. Mo sat in his sandpit while Nan swept the tiles. When Nan had swept the tiles she yelled to Mo, “Time to get in the tub!” Mo did not want to scrub in the tub. He still wanted to sit in his sandpit. “Mo, you must get in the tub because you are full of sand,” said Nan. “OK, fine,” said Mo. He picked up his stuff from the sandpit and got in the tub. Mo had a good scrub so he pulled up the plug. He sat with Nan. “Can I tell you something, Nan? The scrub was not so bad. It got rid of all the sand just like you said.” Nan gave Mo a big hug and a kiss.
