5: New Concept

Introduction

We have learned several ending patterns: -ild, -old, -ind, -olt, -ost with long vowels; Y at the end of a word spelling /ī/ (my) or /ē/ (happy); and the final stable syllable -le (little). Today we review all of them.

Look at the end of the word first. Is there a Y? One syllable: /ī/; two syllables: /ē/. Is there -le? Divide before the consonant. Is there -old, -ind...? Try the long vowel.
