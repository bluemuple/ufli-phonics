5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter E. E is a vowel. The letter E spells the /ĕ/ sound like at the beginning of edge.

E can come at the beginning of a word, such as end and Ed. E can also come in the middle of a word, such as men and get.

Grapheme placement (slides)

men/get/e ; end/Ed/e

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /ĕ/ sound as in 'edge', open your mouth a little wider than for /ĭ/ and keep your tongue relaxed... (model). Be sure your voice is on. /ĕ/ is a continuous sound. Watch me /ĕĕĕ/. You try /ĕĕĕ/.

Sound Wall

Add 'e' under /ĕ/ on the vowel valley.

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase E and lowercase e.
