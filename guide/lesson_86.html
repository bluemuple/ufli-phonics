Instructional Notes

OA spells /ō/ at the beginning or middle of a word (oat, boat). OW spells /ō/ at the end of a word or syllable (show, grow, yellow) - OW also spells /ow/ (cow), Lesson 96. OE spells /ō/ at the end of a few words (toe, doe, goes).

5: New Concept

Introduction

We know that /ō/ can be spelled o in an open syllable (go, cozy) and o_e (bone). Today we will learn three vowel teams that spell /ō/.

OA spells /ō/ like in boat and road. OA comes at the beginning or in the middle of a word. OW spells /ō/ like in show and grow. OW comes at the end of a word or syllable, such as low, grown, yellow. OE spells /ō/ at the end of a few words: toe, hoe, doe, goes.

OW can also spell another sound, /ow/ as in cow. We will learn that later. If /ō/ does not make a real word, try /ow/.

A Trip to the Coast

Harvey owns a big yellow boat. Every summer, he takes the boat on a trip to the coast. This time, he wants to bring his friends, Layla and Joe, with him. There is just one problem, Layla can not swim! Layla must be able to swim if she wants to go on the boat. Layla started swim lessons to grow her skills. She even got a swim coach to teach her. She started slow, but Joe helped her get better. After four weeks of lessons, Layla could swim! Now she could paddle, kick, and float all by herself. Layla showed Harvey her skills. Harvey was thrilled! The next day, Harvey and Layla and Joe set sail to the coast.
