5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter I. I is a vowel. The letter I spells the /ĭ/ sound like at the beginning of itch.

I can come at the beginning of a word, such as in and if. I can also come in the middle of a word, such as fit and dip.

Grapheme placement (slides)

fit/dip/i ; in/if/i

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /ĭ/ sound as in 'itch', open your mouth just a little and smile slightly... (model). Be sure your voice is on. /ĭ/ is a continuous sound that can be stretched out. Watch me /ĭĭĭ/. You try /ĭĭĭ/.

Sound Wall

Add 'i' under /ĭ/ on the vowel valley.

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase I and lowercase i.
