5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter R. R is a consonant. The letter R spells the /r/ sound like at the beginning of red.

R comes at the beginning of a word, such as rat and run. In this lesson R is only at the beginning; next lesson we will read R after another consonant (crab, frog).

Grapheme placement (slides)

rat/run/r

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /r/ sound as in 'red', pull your tongue up and back a little without touching the roof of your mouth, and round your lips slightly... (model). Be sure your voice is on. /r/ can be stretched a little. Watch me /rrr/. You try /rrr/. Do not say /ruh/.

Sound Wall

Add 'r' under /r/ (tongue lifted).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase R and lowercase r.
