w /w/: The grapheme W spells /w/

Instructional Notes

W is only used at the beginning of a word or syllable. At the end of words /w/ is not spelled with w alone.

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter W. W is a consonant. The letter W spells the /w/ sound like at the beginning of watermelon.

W comes at the beginning of a word, such as win and wag. W can also come after another consonant at the beginning, such as swim and twin.

Grapheme placement (slides)

win/wag/w

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /w/ sound as in 'watermelon', round your lips like you are going to whistle, then open them as your voice comes out... (model). Be sure your voice is on. Watch me /w/. You try /w/.

Sound Wall

Add 'w' under /w/ (lips rounded).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase W and lowercase w.
