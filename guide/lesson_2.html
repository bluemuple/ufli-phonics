5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter M. M is a consonant. The letter M spells the /m/ sound like at the beginning of mouse.

M can come at the beginning of a word, such as mad and mop. M can also come at the end of a word, such as gum and him.

Grapheme placement (slides)

mad/mop/m ; gum/him/m

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /m/ sound as in 'mouse', put your lips together and hum like this... (model). Be sure your voice is on. /m/ is a continuous sound that can be stretched out (use continuous hand motion). Watch me /mmm/. You try /mmm/.

Sound Wall

Add 'm' under /m/ (lips together).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase M and lowercase m.
