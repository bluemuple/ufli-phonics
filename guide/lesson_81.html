ir, ur /er/: The graphemes IR and UR spell /er/

Instructional Notes

IR and UR spell the same sound as ER: /er/. IR is usually in the middle of a word (bird, girl, first). UR can be in the middle (turn, hurt) or at the end (fur, blur). Spelling /er/ requires remembering the word - see Lesson 82.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We learned that ER spells /er/, as in her and fern. Today we will learn two more spellings for the same sound. IR spells /er/, like in bird and girl. UR spells /er/, like in turn and hurt.

IR usually comes in the middle of a word: girl, bird, sir, stir. UR comes in the middle (turn, hurt, urge) or at the end of a word (fur, blur). When you hear /er/, it could be spelled er, ir or ur - you have to remember which one.
