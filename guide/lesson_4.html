5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter T. T is a consonant. The letter T spells the /t/ sound like at the beginning of tiger.

T can come at the beginning of a word, such as tap and top. T can also come at the end of a word, such as at and sat.

Grapheme placement (slides)

at/sat/t ; tap/top/t

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /t/ sound as in 'tiger', put the tip of your tongue behind your top teeth and pop the air out... (model). Your voice is off. /t/ is a quick, stop sound. Do not add /uh/ after it. Watch me /t/. You try /t/.

Sound Wall

Add 't' under /t/ (tongue behind top teeth).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase T and lowercase t.
