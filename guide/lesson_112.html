air, are, ear /air/: AIR, ARE and EAR spell /air/

Instructional Notes

Three spellings of /air/: AIR (hair, chair), ARE (care, square) and EAR (bear, wear, pear). EAR also spells /ear/ (hear) - Lesson 113 - so be flexible: try /ear/ first, then /air/.

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we will learn a new r-controlled vowel sound, /air/, like in hair and chair. It has three common spellings.

AIR spells /air/: hair, pair, chair, stairs. ARE spells /air/ at the end of a word: care, share, square. EAR spells /air/ in a few words: bear, wear, pear, tear.

EAR can also spell /ear/ as in hear (we learn that next lesson). When you read a word with EAR, try both sounds to find the real word.

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /air/ sound as in 'hair', open your mouth a little as if for /ĕ/ and glide toward /ə/... (model). Be sure your voice is on. Watch me /air/. You try /air/.

Sound Wall

Add 'air', 'are' and 'ear' under /air/ (r-controlled vowels).

A Smart Pair of Pants

Clare went shopping for a new pair of pants. After going to five stores, she could not find the pair she wanted. Then, at the sixth store, she found them. The pair she picked flared out at the bottom. They were light as air and so comfortable. When she put them on, she stared at herself in the mirror. Clare felt so fantastic in these pants that she twirled around and tossed her hair. She wanted to purchase two pairs so she would have a spare pair to wear. She brought the pants to the checkout counter. “Those are nice pairs of pants,” said the clerk, “if you take good care of them, they will last forever.” “Thank you,” answered Clare with a big smile, “I sure will.”
