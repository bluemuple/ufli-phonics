5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter H. H is a consonant. The letter H spells the /h/ sound like at the beginning of hat.

H comes at the beginning of a word, such as had and has. H does not spell /h/ at the end of a word.

Grapheme placement (slides)

had/has/h

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /h/ sound as in 'hat', open your mouth and push a puff of air from the back of your throat... (model). Your voice is off. Watch me /h/. You try /h/.

Sound Wall

Add 'h' under /h/ (back of throat).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase H and lowercase h.
