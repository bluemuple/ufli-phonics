5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter F. F is a consonant. The letter F spells the /f/ sound like at the beginning of fish.

F can come at the beginning of a word, such as fun and fit. Later we will learn that /f/ at the end of a word is usually spelled ff (off).

Grapheme placement (slides)

fun/fit/f

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /f/ sound as in 'fish', put your top teeth on your bottom lip and blow air out... (model). Your voice is off because this is a quiet sound. /f/ is a continuous sound that can be stretched out. Watch me /fff/. You try /fff/.

Sound Wall

Add 'f' under /f/ (teeth on lip).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase F and lowercase f.
