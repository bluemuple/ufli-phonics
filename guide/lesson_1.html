5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter A. A is a vowel. The letter A spells the /ă/ sound like at the beginning of apple.

A can come at the beginning of a word, such as at and as. A can also come in the middle of a word, such as cat and map.

Grapheme placement (slides)

cat/map/a ; at/as/a

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /ă/ sound as in 'apple', open your mouth wide like this... (model). Be sure your voice is on. Like all vowels, /ă/ is a continuous sound that can be stretched out (use continuous hand motion). Watch me /ăăă/. You try /ăăă/.

Sound Wall

Add 'a' under /ă/ on the vowel valley.
