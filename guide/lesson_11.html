Nasalized A: Nasalized A: -am and -an

Instructional Notes

When /ă/ is followed by /m/ or /n/, the vowel sound changes a little - it becomes nasal. Students may hear it as a different vowel. Teach am and an as chunks.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We know that A spells /ă/. When A comes right before M or N, the /ă/ sound changes a little. Listen: am, an. The air goes through the nose, so we call it nasalized A.

Today we will read and spell words that end with -am, such as Sam and Pam, and words that end with -an, such as fan and tan.

Articulatory Gesture

Say /ă/ then /m/: /ăm/. Feel the air move through your nose at the end. Now /ă/ then /n/: /ăn/.

Sound Wall

Add 'am' and 'an' cards beside /ă/ as needed.
