5: New Concept

Introduction

We have learned four letters and their sounds: a, m, s, t. Today we will use them to read and spell words. Some words have a vowel and a consonant (VC), like at. Some words have a consonant, a vowel and a consonant (CVC), like mat.

To read a word, we say each sound and then blend the sounds together: /mmmăăăt/, mat. To spell a word, we say the word, count the sounds, and write a letter for each sound.
