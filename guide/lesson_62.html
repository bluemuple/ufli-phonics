VCe Review 3, Exceptions: VCe review 3 and VCe exceptions (have, give, love, come, some, done)

Instructional Notes

Some very common words look like VCe words but the vowel is short: have, give, live. Words ending in -ve always have an e (English words do not end in v). In come, some, love, done, none the O spells /ŭ/. Teach these as heart words.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We have learned that the VCe pattern makes the vowel say its name: bake, like, note, and that -ce spells /s/ and -ge spells /j/. Today we review all of these.

We will also learn some exceptions. Some VCe words do not follow the rule. In have, give and live the vowel is short. In come, some, love, dove, done and none the O spells /ŭ/. These words end in e because English words do not end in v, or because of their history. We learn them by heart.
