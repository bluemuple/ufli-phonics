Instructional Notes

In -all, -oll and -ull the vowel does not say its short sound: all /orl/, roll /rōl/, pull /pool/. Teach them as chunks (word families). In Australian/NZ English -all sounds like /orl/.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We learned that L is doubled at the end of a word after a short vowel (bell, hill). Today we look at three special endings: -all, -oll and -ull. In these words the vowel does not say its usual short sound.

-all says /orl/ as in ball and small. -oll usually says /ōl/ as in roll and troll (but doll has short o). -ull says /ool/ as in pull and full (but dull has short u). Read these endings as chunks.
