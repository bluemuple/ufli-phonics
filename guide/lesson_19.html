5: New Concept

Introduction

We have learned all five short vowels: a /ă/, e /ĕ/, i /ĭ/, o /ŏ/, u /ŭ/. Every word has a vowel sound. Today we practise reading and spelling words with all of them.

When you read, look at the vowel first and think about its sound; then say each sound and blend.

Articulatory Gesture

Review all five short vowel gestures using the vowel valley (mouth opens wider from /ĭ/ to /ĕ/ to /ă/; /ŏ/ round and open; /ŭ/ relaxed).

Sound Wall

No new grapheme today.
