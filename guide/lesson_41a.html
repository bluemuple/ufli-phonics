5: New Concept

Introduction

We are reviewing all five short vowels. Every word has a vowel. Look at the vowel, think of its sound, then say each sound and blend.

Decodable Text (from the slides)

The Fox and the Red Hen

The fox can not get up. He is in a pit. The red hen looks at the fox. “Oh no! You are in the pit.” “Yes, I am in the pit. Can you help me?” asks the fox. “Yes, I can help, but do not jump on me!” said the red hen. The red hen helps the fox. The fox did not jump on the red hen. The red hen and the fox are glad the fox is not in the pit.
