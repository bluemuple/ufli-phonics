5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter S. S is a consonant. The letter S spells the /s/ sound like at the beginning of sun.

S can come at the beginning of a word, such as sat and sip. S can also come at the end of a word, such as bus and gas.

Grapheme placement (slides)

sat/sip/s

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /s/ sound as in 'sun', put your teeth together, keep your lips open and push air through like a snake... (model). Your voice is off because this is a quiet sound. /s/ is a continuous sound that can be stretched out. Watch me /sss/. You try /sss/.

Sound Wall

Add 's' under /s/ (tongue behind top teeth).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase S and lowercase s.
