tch /ch/: The grapheme TCH spells /ch/

Instructional Notes

Use TCH right after a short vowel at the end of a word (catch, itch). Use CH after a consonant or a long vowel (bench, much, coach). Exceptions: much, such, rich, which, sandwich.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We know that CH spells /ch/ as in chip and much. Today we will learn another way to spell /ch/: TCH. T-C-H spells one sound, /ch/, like at the end of catch.

TCH comes at the end of a word right after a short vowel: match, fetch, itch, witch. After a consonant we use CH: bench, lunch. This is like CK: right after a short vowel we use ck (back), and after a consonant we use k (milk).

A few words are exceptions and use CH after a short vowel: much, such, rich, which.
