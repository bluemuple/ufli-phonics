5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter U. U is a vowel. The letter U spells the /ŭ/ sound like at the beginning of up.

U can come at the beginning of a word, such as up and us. U can also come in the middle of a word, such as nut and fun.

Grapheme placement (slides)

nut/fun/u ; up/us/u

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /ŭ/ sound as in 'up', open your mouth a little and relax your tongue and lips... (model). Be sure your voice is on. /ŭ/ is a continuous sound. Watch me /ŭŭŭ/. You try /ŭŭŭ/.

Sound Wall

Add 'u' under /ŭ/ on the vowel valley.

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase U and lowercase u.
