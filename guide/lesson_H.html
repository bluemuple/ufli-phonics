Getting Ready Lesson H: Vowel sounds ordered by how open the mouth is

1: Sound Wall

Vowels: the vowel valley

The vowel valley shows the vowel sounds in order. On the left side the mouth starts almost closed with a smile (/ē/) and opens wider and wider (/ĭ/, /ā/, /ĕ/, /ă/). At the bottom of the valley the mouth is wide open (/ī/, /ŭ/, /ŏ/). Going up the right side the lips get rounder and rounder (/ō/, /oo/, /yū/, /ū/).

Say the vowels in order and feel your mouth open and then round: /ē/ /ĭ/ /ā/ /ĕ/ /ă/ /ī/ /ŭ/ /ŏ/ /ō/ /oo/ /yū/ /ū/.

2: Alphabet Knowledge & Letter Formation

Letters: K, M, N, A

Uppercase K: down, then two slanted lines meeting in the middle.

Uppercase M: down, slant down, slant up, down.

Uppercase N: down, slant down, up.

Uppercase A: slant down to the left, slant down to the right, across the middle.

Use the letter-formation animations in the slides: "Watch me write", then "Let's write together". Practise with sky writing, tracing and whiteboards.

Routines to establish

Choral response signal; "My turn / Your turn"; whiteboard "3, 2, 1, show me"; hand gestures for continuous (stretch), stop (chop), voiced (touch throat) and unvoiced sounds.
