er /er/: The grapheme ER spells /er/

Instructional Notes

ER spells /er/ in the middle of a word (fern, verb) and at the end (her). Lesson 80 AUS covers ER as the unstressed schwa /ə/ at the end of two-syllable words (letter, sister).

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning another r-controlled vowel. When E is followed by R, the R changes the vowel sound. ER spells /er/, like in her and fern.

ER can come in the middle of a word, such as verb and fern. ER can also come at the end of a word, such as her and per.

Grapheme placement (slides)

verb/fern/er ; her/per/er

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /er/ sound as in 'earth', open your mouth a little and pull your tongue back and up slightly... (model). Be sure your voice is on. /er/ is a continuous sound. Watch me /er/. You try /er/.

Sound Wall

Add 'er' under /er/.

Clara’s Garden

Clara is an expert at taking care of plants, herbs and ferns. In her garden, Clara plants many herbs and waters them every day. Clara serves herbs to the village when cooking for them. The herbs add a very nice perk to every meal. At the edge of her garden, Clara sees her purple potted fern. It was close to being an extinct fern so she wanted to preserve it. Next to the fern, she noticed a small herd of slugs. The slugs liked to perch next to the purple fern. Clara picked up the potted fern as she did not want the slugs to attack her fern. She swerved across the garden and put the potted fern on a ledge. Every herb, fern and plant was important to her. Clara preserved her garden well.
