5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we will learn the suffix -ing. When we add -ing to a verb, it tells us the action is happening now, or is going on: I am jumping. She is reading.

We often use a helping verb with -ing: am, is, are, was, were. I am going. He is going. We are going. She was going. They were going.

-ing is one syllable: /ĭng/. When you read a word that ends in -ing, cover the -ing, read the base word, then add the ending: jump-ing, jumping.

Articulatory Gesture

Review /ŋ/ (tongue back, voice through the nose).
