_ce /s/: _ce spells /s/ (soft c)

Instructional Notes

C spells /s/ when it is followed by e (also i and y - Lesson 117). Words ending in -ce: the e signals the soft c and often also makes the vowel long (race, ice), but not always (dance, fence, since).

5: New Concept

Introduction

We know that C spells /k/ as in cat. Today we will learn that C can also spell /s/. When C is followed by E, it spells /s/, like at the end of race and ice.

-ce comes at the end of a word: race, nice, place. The e is silent. It tells the C to say /s/, and it often makes the vowel long: r-a-ce, race. In some words the vowel stays short: dance, fence.
