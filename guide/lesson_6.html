5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter P. P is a consonant. The letter P spells the /p/ sound like at the beginning of pig.

P can come at the beginning of a word, such as pot and pig. P can also come at the end of a word, such as hop and nap.

Grapheme placement (slides)

pot/pig/p ; hop/nap/p

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /p/ sound as in 'pig', put your lips together and pop them open with a puff of air... (model). Your voice is off. /p/ is a quick, stop sound. Watch me /p/. You try /p/.

Sound Wall

Add 'p' under /p/ (lips together).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase P and lowercase p.
