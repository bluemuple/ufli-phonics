5: New Concept

Introduction

We have learned the letters a, i, f, m, n, p, s, t. Today we will practise reading and spelling words with the short vowels /ă/ and /ĭ/. Remember: say each sound, then blend the sounds together.

Articulatory Gesture

Review the articulatory gestures for /ă/ and /ĭ/. Have students feel the difference in mouth opening between /ă/ (wide) and /ĭ/ (small smile).
