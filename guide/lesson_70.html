dge /j/: The grapheme DGE spells /j/

Instructional Notes

Use DGE right after a short vowel at the end of a word (badge, bridge, fudge). Use -GE after a consonant or long vowel (cage, hinge, large). Like ck and tch, dge follows a short vowel.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We know that -ge spells /j/ at the end of a word after a long vowel: cage, huge. Today we will learn DGE. D-G-E spells one sound, /j/, like at the end of badge.

DGE comes at the end of a word right after a short vowel: badge, edge, bridge, fudge. This is like ck and tch: right after a short vowel we use the longer spelling. After a consonant or a long vowel we use -ge: hinge, cage.

Remember: J is never written at the end of a word. We use -ge or -dge.
