Unvoiced th /th/: The consonant digraph TH spells unvoiced /th/

Instructional Notes

The same digraph TH spells the quiet /th/ (voice off) in thin, bath, moth. Have students feel their throat to tell the two sounds apart.

5: New Concept

Introduction

Yesterday we learned that TH spells the /th/ sound with the voice on, as in this and that. Today we will learn that TH can also spell a quiet /th/ sound, with the voice off, as in thin and thick.

Put your tongue between your teeth and blow air out quietly - /th/. TH can come at the beginning of a word, such as thin and thick, or at the end of a word, such as with and moth.

Grapheme placement (slides)

thin/thick/th ; with/moth/th

Articulatory Gesture

To make the unvoiced /th/ sound, put your tongue between your teeth and blow air out with your voice off... (model). It is a quiet sound. /th/ is a continuous sound. Watch me /th/. You try /th/. Now compare: /th/ (this) with voice on, /th/ (thin) with voice off.

Sound Wall

Add 'th' under unvoiced /th/ (tongue between teeth).
