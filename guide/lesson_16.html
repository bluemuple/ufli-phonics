5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letter G. G is a consonant. The letter G spells the /g/ sound like at the beginning of goat.

G can come at the beginning of a word, such as got and gas. G can also come at the end of a word, such as dog and tag.

Grapheme placement (slides)

got/gas/g ; dog/tag/g

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /g/ sound as in 'goat', pull the back of your tongue up to the back of the roof of your mouth and pop the air out with your voice on... (model). It is like /k/ with the voice on. /g/ is a quick, stop sound. Watch me /g/. You try /g/.

Sound Wall

Add 'g' under /g/ (tongue pulled back).

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase G and lowercase g.
