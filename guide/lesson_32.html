Instructional Notes

Q is always followed by U. Together they spell two sounds, /k/ + /w/. Count /kw/ as two sounds when segmenting.

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letters QU. The letter Q is always followed by the letter U. Together QU spells the sounds /k/ /w/, like at the beginning of quilt.

QU comes at the beginning of a word, such as quiz and quilt. It can also come after S, such as squid.

Grapheme placement (slides)

quiz/quilt/qu

Articulatory Gesture

Say /k/ then /w/ quickly: /kw/. Pop the back of your tongue for /k/, then round your lips for /w/. Watch me /kw/. You try /kw/.

Sound Wall

Add 'qu' under /k/ and /w/ with a note that it spells both sounds.

Letter Formation

Practise letter formation for uppercase Q and lowercase q.
