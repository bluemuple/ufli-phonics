5: New Concept

Introduction

We know that L spells /l/ at the beginning of a word. L can also come right after another consonant at the beginning of a word: f-l-a-g, flag. Say every sound: /f/ /l/ /ă/ /g/.

Today we will read and spell words that begin with two consonants ending in L, such as slip, glad, clap and plan.
