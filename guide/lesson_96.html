ou, ow /ow/: The diphthongs OU and OW spell /ow/

Instructional Notes

OU spells /ow/ at the beginning or middle of a word (out, loud, house). OW spells /ow/ at the end of a word (how, cow), before n, l (down, owl, town) and in a few others (crowd). OW also spells /ō/ (snow) - be flexible.

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we will learn another diphthong, /ow/, like in owl and cow. Your mouth starts open and glides to round: /a/ /oo/, /ow/.

OU spells /ow/ at the beginning or middle of a word: out, loud, house, round. OW spells /ow/ at the end of a word (how, cow, now) and before n or l (down, town, owl, growl).

Remember OW can also spell /ō/ (snow, grow). Try /ō/ and /ow/ to see which makes a real word.

Grapheme placement (slides)

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /ow/ sound as in 'owl', open your mouth wide and glide to round lips... (model). Be sure your voice is on. Feel your lips move. Watch me /ow/. You try /ow/.

Sound Wall

Summer at the Farmhouse

Joey is staying with his grandmother for the summer. His grandmother owns a farmhouse close to town. Joey likes to help his grandmother around the farm. However, he most enjoys watching the animals who live outdoors. On Monday, he saw a brown owl hunting a small mouse. The mouse found its way back underground just before the owl swooped down. On Wednesday, he walked down to the stream south of the farm. This stream is full of trout and Joey likes to watch them swim. Then on Friday, he went to the meadow where the cows graze. He picked some wildflowers for his grandmother. Joey hopes to return to his grandmother’s farmhouse each summer.
