5: New Concept

Introduction

We have learned that after a short vowel at the end of a word, /ch/ is spelled tch (catch) and /j/ is spelled dge (badge). After a consonant or a long vowel we use ch and ge (bench, cage). Today we review both.

When you spell a word ending in /ch/ or /j/, ask: is the vowel short and right before the sound? If yes, use tch or dge.
