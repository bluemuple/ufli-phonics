ck /k/: The grapheme CK spells /k/

Instructional Notes

CK is used right after a short vowel at the end of a one-syllable word (back, neck). After a consonant, use k (desk, milk). CK never begins a word.

5: New Concept

Introduction

Today we are learning about the letters CK. C and K both spell /k/. When they come together, they spell one sound, /k/, like at the end of duck.

CK comes at the end of a word right after a short vowel, such as rock and black. If there is another consonant before the /k/, we use just K (desk, milk). CK never comes at the beginning of a word.
