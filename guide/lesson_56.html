5: New Concept

Introduction

We know the VCe pattern with a_e and i_e. Today we will learn o_e. O, consonant, silent e: the O says /ō/, its name, like in home.

Look: hop. Add the silent e: hope. h-o-p-e, hope. Words with o_e: rope, nose, note, home.

Articulatory Gesture

To make the /ō/ sound as in 'open', round your lips and glide... (model). Be sure your voice is on. /ō/ is a continuous sound. Watch me /ōōō/. You try /ōōō/.

Sound Wall

Add 'o_e' under /ō/ on the vowel valley.

Decodable Text (from the slides)

The Drive Home

Hope and Dad went to the lake for a swim. They swam and swam. “It’s time to go home, Hope,” Dad yelled. As Dad drove home, Hope could see smoke! “Dad there is smoke!” yells Hope. “It is next to the phone shop.” They drove fast to the phone shop. When they got to the shop they froze. The shop was on fire! Dad calls 000. “Yes, send the fire trucks!” Dad tells the man on the line. While Dad is on the phone, Hope sees a man run out of the shop. He is safe. The fire trucks came just in time to get rid of the flames. Hope and Dad save the shop!
