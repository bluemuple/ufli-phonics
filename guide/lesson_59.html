5: New Concept

Introduction

We have learned the VCe pattern with all five vowels: a_e /ā/, i_e /ī/, o_e /ō/, e_e /ē/, u_e /ū/ /yū/. Today we practise reading and spelling all of them.

When you see a vowel, one consonant and silent e at the end, the vowel says its name. Look at the vowel first, then read: sh-a-p-e, shape.
