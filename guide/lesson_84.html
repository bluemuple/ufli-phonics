ai, ay/ā/: The vowel teams AI and AY spell /ā/

Instructional Notes

A vowel team is two or more letters that work together to spell one vowel sound. AI spells /ā/ at the beginning or in the middle of a word (aim, rain). AY spells /ā/ at the end of a word or syllable (day, playing).

We now have four spellings of /ā/: a (open syllable), a_e, ai, ay.

5: New Concept

Introduction

A vowel team is two or more letters that work together to make one sound. We have already learned some ways to spell /ā/: a in an open syllable (baby) and a_e (cake). Today we will learn two vowel teams that spell /ā/.

AI spells /ā/ like in rain. AI comes at the beginning or in the middle of a word: aim, wait, paint. AY also spells /ā/ like in day. AY comes at the end of a word: say, play, stay.

When you hear /ā/ at the end of a word, use AY. In the middle, use AI or a_e.

Decodable Text (from the slides)

Sunday Fun

On Sundays, Gail visits her brother at his farm. Gail loves to go to the farm because there is so much to do. She starts the day in the garden. If it did not rain, she sprays the plants with water. Then, Gail and her brother walk on the trail from the garden to the pond. As they walk, Gail hunts for snail shells. When she finds shells, she tucks them in her pocket. She will paint them when she gets home. At the pond, her brother likes to fish. He strings bait onto his fishing rod and waits for a bite. While they wait, Gail plays in the mud and clay. At the end of the day, they walk back up the trail to go home. As they walk, they see the sun set and the day fade away.
