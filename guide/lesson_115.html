Alternate Long U: Alternate spellings of long U: ew, eu, ue /yū/ and ou /ū/

Instructional Notes

EW and UE can spell /yū/ as well as /ū/: few, nephew, dew (/yū/) vs. new, blew (/ū/ in most Australian/NZ speech 'new' is /nyū/); cue, rescue, value (/yū/) vs. blue, glue (/ū/). EU spells /ū/ or /yū/ in a few words (neutral, feud). OU spells /ū/ in you, soup, group, youth.

5: New Concept

Introduction

We know that EW, UI and UE spell /ū/ (new, fruit, blue), and that u_e can spell /ū/ or /yū/ (rule, cute). Today we will learn that EW and UE can also spell /yū/, the name of the letter U.

EW spells /yū/ in few, dew, nephew, curfew. UE spells /yū/ in cue, rescue, value, argue. EU is a rare spelling: neutral (/ū/), feud (/yū/). When you read ew or ue, try /ū/ and /yū/ to find the real word.

OU can also spell /ū/ in a few words: you, soup, group, youth. This is another sound for OU, which usually spells /ow/ (out).

Decodable Text (from the slides)

It’s No Fun to Argue

Stewart lives down the street from his nephew, Louis. They love to spend time together. Stewart calls Louis the best nephew on earth and Louis calls Stewart the best uncle on earth. Last Saturday, Stewart took Louis to the park to play. Louis played a new game with a group of his friends. The game was going well, but then a few of the kids started to argue about the rules. The game could not continue while the kids were arguing. This made the rest of the group angry. After a few minutes, Stewart came to the rescue. He said, “if you all continue to argue, you will not have any time left to play.” This made everyone stop and think. The kids agreed that they wanted to spend their time playing instead of arguing. “See,” Louis yelled, “this is why Stewart is the best uncle on earth!”
